# Form checks, pacing, and model-training status

Rebound uses the existing pretrained MediaPipe Pose model. This update adds deterministic checks to its estimated landmarks, not newly trained model weights. No user or clinic clip is collected for training. Permission to send a clip to a clinician is not permission to train on it.

Supported checks include visibility/framing/view, trunk lean, curl upper-arm drift, broad wall-press alignment, unexpected limb bending for shoulder lifts/sideways leg lifts, and minimum cycle timing. Uncertain observations pause counts. Feedback describes estimates, never certifies correct or safe form. Modified clinician-taught movements can legitimately differ; use manual counting in that case rather than forcing the illustrated position. The model cannot measure pain, force, band tension, grip, balance support, or joint mechanics.

Camera pacing arms after usable tracking. Eight seconds without at least four degrees of cumulative angle change pauses counting and active time; three seconds without usable tracking also pauses. Tiny angle jitter does not reset the stillness deadline. Resume is explicit and resets the rep cycle, discarding unfinished movement. This is for supported cyclic movements, not an isometric-hold assessment. Manual and hold exercises do not infer stillness from a body-pose model.

Breaks are user-controlled: choose a duration, optionally enter reps between breaks from an existing plan, or take a break manually. No rep interval is prescribed by default. A break pauses active time/counting, uses a monotonic deadline, and waits for explicit resume after expiry. Ending a camera view releases the camera; a short in-session break keeps a visible camera preview running to help the user reposition. Backgrounding/closing still stops camera use.

Before any claim of a trained bad-form classifier, obtain lawfully usable footage with specific training consent and clinician-adjudicated labels, including acceptable individual modifications, injury restrictions, camera view, equipment, and occlusion. Split by participant/session (not neighboring frames), hold out a real evaluation cohort, and report per-exercise sensitivity, false alarms, rep error, and physical-device performance. Include diverse bodies, clothing, cameras, lighting, and clinically cleared ranges. Review unsafe false negatives and overly restrictive false positives with qualified clinicians. Freeze/version the model and thresholds and validate again after changes. No such dataset or clinical validation is available in this repository, so no training or accuracy percentage is claimed.

Synthetic landmark and browser tests verify the implemented rules, pauses, rest deadlines, resume boundaries, and no-count behavior. They do not establish real-person form accuracy or injury prevention.
